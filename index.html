02 — About

Idea to shipped.

I'm Rabi Dahal — proobker on GitHub — a developer based in Bhaktapur, Nepal. I started with small C games and web pages, and now ship full-stack products, Android apps and simulation engines.

My process is hands-on and iterative: prototype quickly, ship it, then rebuild cleaner. Recent work spans an AI-powered social quest game, a women's-safety app, a peer-to-peer aircraft conflict-resolution simulator and a 3D shooter.

06 — Journey

Journey so far.

From first C games to simulation engines — the milestones behind my GitHub, in order.

  1. May 2024

    Foundations

    Completed SEE with a 3.91 GPA at Gladstone Academy. Science expos, Red Cross volunteering and public speaking — where the engineering itch first formed.

  2. Dec 2024

    First commits

    Snake and Hangman in C, plus small web builds — a to-do app, tax calculator, weather checker and an e-commerce mockup.

  3. Aug 2025

    Python & algorithms

    Earned Harvard's CS50P alongside +2 Science at Trinity College. Built an A* pathfinding visualizer for map images and a geography quiz game.

  4. Apr 2026

    Full-stack & mobile

    Shipped Rakshya, a one-tap SOS safety app, then rebuilt it natively in Kotlin as Rakshyaa. Built QST (Next.js, Supabase, Gemini), turning real life into an RPG quest game.

  5. Sep 2026

    Simulations & games

    SkyMesh, a failure-resilient peer-to-peer aircraft conflict-resolution simulator; TerraSim, a terrain simulation engine; and Cracked, a 3D FPS in Godot. Plus hackathon builds.
